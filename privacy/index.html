Privacy notice

Cardinal Landscape Associates LLC · quote.cardinallm.net · Updated October 2026

What we collect

When you request a quote, estimate or site walk on this site we collect what you type into the form: your name, phone number, email, the property address, and the details of the work you're asking about (services, project type, budget range, timing, notes). For residential lawn quotes we also look up the lot size of the address you enter in public county property records.

How we use it

To prepare your quote, contact you about it, schedule the work, and bill for it. Your information is stored in Jobber, the scheduling and invoicing software we run the business on. We do not sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with anyone except the services we use to run the business (Jobber, our email and phone providers, and payment processing if you put a card on file).

Advertising and analytics

This site uses the Google Ads tag and the Meta (Facebook) pixel so we can tell which ads bring people here and show you relevant ads later. These tools set cookies and may collect your IP address and the pages you visit. You can opt out through Google Ads Settings and Facebook Ad Preferences, or by using your browser's cookie controls.

Texts and calls

If you give us your phone number we may call or text you about your quote and your scheduled work. Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving them.
